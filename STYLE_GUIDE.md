# Ryan's Personal Style Guide

_Used internally by Claude when building outfits, evaluating new items, and identifying purchase gaps._

---

## Aesthetic Identity

**"Relaxed Ivy / Mediterranean Ease"** — The effortless elegance of someone who doesn't try hard but clearly knows what they're doing. Relaxed silhouettes in warm natural tones. Textured blazers over quality tees. Loafers as the default shoe. Looks like it was thrown on in five minutes and took fifteen years to understand.

Two modes that share the same DNA:
- **Mediterranean warm-weather**: Linen, cream, sand, camp collar, wide-leg trouser, loafer or sandal
- **Relaxed Ivy cool-weather**: Tweed/herringbone blazer, cream tee, dark indigo relaxed jeans, brown suede loafer, waxed jacket, vintage watch

Style references (pinned):
- Cream linen unstructured blazer + white tee + wide-leg trouser + chunky loafer
- Check/houndstooth blazer (oversized) + white tee + relaxed jeans + brown loafer + navy cap
- Wide-leg pleated trouser + mac coat/trench + graphic tee + chunky loafer
- Oatmeal sweater + stripe shirt collar + olive waxed jacket + navy chino + brown suede loafer + vintage watch
- Camp collar print shirt (open) + white tank + relaxed wide-leg trouser + leather sandal

## The Silhouette (most important rule)

**Relaxed and wide on the bottom, fitted or slightly relaxed on top.**

This is the non-negotiable. Every reference image uses a wide-leg or pleated trouser — high-waisted, roomy through the thigh, with a clean break at the ankle. Not slim. Not skinny. Not athletic taper.

The top half stays fitted or slightly relaxed — a quality tee, an open check shirt, an unstructured blazer. The volume lives in the trousers, not the top.

**Current wardrobe problem:** Almost every trouser and chino is slim or athletic fit. This is the single biggest gap between current wardrobe and target aesthetic.

## Color System

| Color | Role |
|-------|------|

| **Stone / oatmeal** | Sweaters, layering pieces |
| **Olive / earth green** | Casual tops, outerwear, accent trousers |
| **Brown / cognac** | Shoes, belts, leather goods — essential warmth |

### Accent Colors
| Color | Use |
|-------|-----|
| **White** | Quality tees and shirts — the anchor of almost every look |
| **Warm grey** | Wide-leg trousers, casual trousers |
| **Navy** | Shirts and blazers only — used sparingly |
| **Glen plaid / check** | Pattern via trousers or shirts in warm tones |

### Black: Demoted to Accent Only
Black is not the foundation of this aesthetic. It works as an occasional outerwear piece (mac coat) or shoe. It should not be the default choice for trousers, jeans, or tops when going out. The current wardrobe is over-indexed on black by a wide margin.

## Style Rules

1. **Relaxed bottom, fitted top** — volume in the trouser, not the shirt. Always.
2. **White tee is the anchor** — a quality white tee is the base of more looks than any other single piece. Invest here first.
3. **Loafer or leather shoe as default** — sneakers are a deliberate casual choice, not the default. The reference images have loafers or leather in 4 of 5 looks.
4. **Warm tones over cold** — when choosing between navy and cream, cream. Between black and tan, tan. The palette runs warm.
5. **Pattern is allowed and encouraged** — check shirts, glen plaid, camp collar prints, seersucker. One pattern per outfit, everything else solid.
6. **Nothing buttoned to the top** — open collars, untucked shirts, relaxed layering. Nothing stiff or constructed.
7. **Texture over logo** — seersucker, linen, waffle knit, canvas. Not branding, not graphics.
8. **Accessories complete it** — sunglasses, a good watch, a leather belt. These are not optional in this aesthetic.

## Outfit Architecture

### Dressed Up (dinner, date, event)
```
Top:        Unstructured linen/seersucker blazer over quality white tee
Bottom:     Wide-leg pleated trouser in cream, sand, or warm grey
Shoes:      Chunky loafer (brown leather or tan suede)
Belt:       Brown leather
```

### Going Out (bar, casual dinner)
```
Top:        Quality white tee or open check/linen shirt
Bottom:     Wide-leg or relaxed pleated trouser in khaki, warm grey, or olive
Shoes:      Loafer or brown leather boot
Outerwear:  Long mac coat or trench (tan or black) if needed
```

### Casual (weekend, daytime)
```
Top:        Camp collar shirt (open, worn over white tee or tank) OR check shirt
Bottom:     Relaxed trouser, wide-leg chino, or linen pant
Shoes:      Loafer, sandal/slide, or brown boot
```

### Warm Weather
```
Top:        Camp collar print shirt or linen shirt (open)
Bottom:     Relaxed linen or cotton trouser, or quality shorts (not athletic)
Shoes:      Loafer, leather sandal, or espadrille
```

## What to Stop Buying

- Slim or athletic-fit trousers of any kind
- Black as a default color for going-out pieces
- Performance fabric (Lululemon, Nike) for anything above formality level 1
- More athletic shorts — deeply over-stocked
- Logo or branded athletic wear for going out
- Fast-fashion basics (Zara tops, Old Navy sweaters) as going-out anchors
